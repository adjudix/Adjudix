## 7. Membership Structure (cross-referenced across both documents)

- Two-tier structure explicitly named: **Affiliated Members** and **Associated Members**, framed together as a "community of connection"
- Entry appears to involve a **free membership offered after a verification step** (video call and/or CV review) — i.e., a vetted, no-cost entry tier rather than a paid subscription ladder (contrast with CIArb's paid application-fee-plus-subscription model)
- A separate **Panel of Professionals** is referenced distinctly from "Members" — suggesting Adjudix distinguishes between (a) general community members (Affiliated/Associated) and (b) credentialed panelists who actually sit as arbitrators/mediators/conciliators on cases
- **Apprenticeship & Internship Programme** exists as a related but distinct pipeline (likely for students/early-career candidates, feeding eventually into Panel or Membership status)

**Decision (confirmed with user, Sept 2026):** expand into a fuller ladder rather than keeping only the two named tiers. Final structure to carry into the IA phase: **Affiliated** and **Associated** remain the entry-level/community tiers (free, verification-based, per the notes), with additional higher tiers layered above for career progression and B2B relationships — modeled on CIArb's Student→Associate→Member→Fellow progression plus a distinct **Corporate** membership category for institutional/business clients. Exact tier names/count and entry criteria (assessment/course/experience routes, per `research/competitors/ciarb.md`) to be finalized during the sitemap/IA phase.
