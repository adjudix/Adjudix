Register: Modern Standard Arabic, formal (institutional legal register). Western digits only via {n} slots.
Brand "Adjudix" stays in Latin script; names, emails, URLs, codes (H-001/26), post-nominals, "ADR-i", ₹ amounts stay as-is.
Indian statutes: English title, then a short Arabic gloss in parentheses on first use in a string.
